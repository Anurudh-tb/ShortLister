How it works

  1. Rubric. The model reads the job description and proposes weighted criteria. You review and edit them, including which ones are must-haves.
  2. Scoring. Each resume is scored 0 to 10 on every criterion, with a quote from the resume as evidence.
  3. Verification. Every quote is checked against the resume. Quotes that can't be found are flagged Unverified, and that score's confidence goes down.
  4. Your decision. Candidates are ranked by weighted score, with missing must-haves flagged. You compare, add notes, and shortlist or reject.

Fairness

The model is told to ignore name, gender, age, photo, nationality and marital status. Turn on Blind review (top right) to also hide names and filenames from yourself while reviewing.
